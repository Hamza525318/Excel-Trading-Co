What is the difference between a hex flange nut and a standard hex nut?

A hex flange nut has a wide integrated flange that spreads the clamping load like a built-in washer, and the serrated version has teeth under the flange that grip the surface and resist loosening under vibration. A standard hex nut needs a separate washer to spread the load.

What is an insert press nut used for?

An insert press nut, also called a kalei nut or set nut, is pressed permanently into a pre-punched hole in thin sheet metal to create a strong, reusable thread. It is widely used in switchboards, enclosures, electronics and sheet metal fabrication where welding nuts is not practical.
