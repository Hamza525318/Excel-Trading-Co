What are the specifications of this cordless impact driver?

The cordless impact driver runs on DC 10.8 V, with a no-load speed of 0 to 2400 rpm, a 1/4 inch hex drive, maximum torque of 100 Nm (885 in-lbs) and an impact rate of up to 3000 blows per minute. It weighs 1050 g with the battery.

What is an impact driver used for?

An impact driver is used to drive screws and bolts quickly with high torque, such as self drilling screws, self tapping screws and small bolts, with less effort and wrist strain than a normal drill. The 1/4 inch hex drive accepts standard hex bits and nut setters.
