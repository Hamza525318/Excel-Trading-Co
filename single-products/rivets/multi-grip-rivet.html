Frequently Asked Questions

What is a multi grip blind rivet?

A multi grip blind rivet is a blind rivet designed to cover a wide range of material thicknesses with a single rivet length. Its body forms correctly across the whole grip range, giving optimum clamp-up force, so one part can replace several standard open end rivets and reduce inventory.

What are the advantages of multi grip rivets?

Multi grip rivets give optimum clamp-up force, accommodate oversized and irregular holes, and have high mandrel head retention. Because one rivet covers several thicknesses, they simplify stock and reduce the risk of using the wrong rivet length.

Where are multi grip rivets used?

They are used with plastics, in the automotive industry and in furniture, and anywhere material thickness varies or holes are not perfectly round.

Which materials are multi grip rivets available in?

Multi grip blind rivets are available in steel, stainless steel and aluminium. Choose the material to match the parts being joined and the corrosion conditions.
