Frequently Asked Questions

What is a structural blind tite rivet?

A structural blind tite rivet is a high-strength aluminium blind rivet whose tail splits as it is set, forming a very large bearing area against the rear sheet. That wide footprint resists pull-out and cracking of the work piece and gives strong, vibration resistant joints.

Where are structural blind tite rivets used?

They are ideal for large construction applications and domestic applications, especially where the rear material is thin, soft or brittle and a standard rivet could pull through.

Can one structural rivet replace several standard rivets?

Often, yes. Because the split tail covers a wide grip range and a large bearing area, one structural blind tite rivet can frequently replace several standard grip rivets, simplifying stock and assembly.
