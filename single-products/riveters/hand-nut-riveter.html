Frequently Asked Questions

What is a hand nut riveter?

A hand nut riveter is a manual tool for setting rivet nuts (threaded inserts) in sheet metal, tubes and profiles. The rivet nut is screwed onto the tool's threaded mandrel, inserted into the hole, and the handles are squeezed so the rivet nut collapses and grips the panel. The mandrel is then unscrewed, leaving a strong thread installed from one side.

Which rivet nut sizes can this tool set?

The tool has a mandrel capacity of M3, M4, M5, M6, M8, M10 and M12. It comes with M5, M6 and M8 mandrels as standard accessories, and spare adaptors are available for M3 to M12.

How long is the hand nut riveter?

The tool is 330 mm (13 inches) long. The long handles give good leverage for setting larger rivet nuts by hand.
