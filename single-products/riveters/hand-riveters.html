Frequently Asked Questions

Which hand riveter should I choose?

The HR-901N sets open end aluminium/steel blind rivets from 2.4 to 4.8 mm. The HR-701 sets 2.4 to 4.8 mm rivets in aluminium/steel, steel/steel and, in larger sizes, stainless steel. The HR-702 is for bigger rivets from 3.2 to 6.4 mm, including steel/steel and stainless steel. See the tables on this page for exact rivet compatibility.

What materials can be fastened with a hand riveter?

Hand riveters set blind rivets that can fasten metal, plastic and leather, and they are widely used in the hardware, sheet metal, HVAC and fabrication industries. They create strong, permanent connections from one side of the work.

Do hand riveters need electricity or compressed air?

No. Hand riveters are fully manual, so they can be used anywhere, including on site and in remote locations without electricity or compressed air. For high-volume production, a pneumatic riveter is faster.
