Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the AR 600 and AR 800 pneumatic riveters?

The AR 600 has a 15.5 mm stroke and 800 kgf pull force at 6 bar, weighs 1.5 kg and uses about 1.5 litres of air per stroke. The AR 800 has an 18 mm stroke and 1250 kgf pull force at 6 bar, weighs 2 kg and uses about 2.9 litres of air per stroke. The AR 800 is the better choice for steel and stainless steel rivets that need more pulling force.

What air supply does a pneumatic riveter need?

Both models work at an air pressure of 71 to 85 psi, with a 1/4 inch NPT air inlet and a 3/8 inch inside diameter air hose. Use clean, dry, lubricated compressed air for long tool life.

Which rivets can these pneumatic riveters set?

They set blind rivets of 2.4, 3.2, 4.0 and 4.8 mm (3/32 to 3/16 inch) in aluminium/steel, steel/steel and, in most sizes, stainless steel. See the compatibility tables on this page for each model.
