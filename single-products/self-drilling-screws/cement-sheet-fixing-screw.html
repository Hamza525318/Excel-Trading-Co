Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cement sheet fixing screw?

A cement sheet fixing screw is a self drilling screw designed to fix fibre cement sheets to steel purlins and frames. It has wings above the drill point that ream a clearance hole in the cement sheet, so the sheet is not cracked, and an EPDM washer that seals the fixing against water.

Why do cement sheet screws have wings?

The wings cut a slightly oversize hole through the fibre cement sheet before breaking off when they reach the steel, so the thread engages only the steel. This stops the sheet from being lifted or cracked and improves drilling performance for exterior fixing.

Which sizes are available?

The screws are #14-20 (M6.3) in 90, 100 and 110 mm lengths, with a #5 drill point and a drill capacity of 12 mm steel. They are available in white zinc and stainless steel.
