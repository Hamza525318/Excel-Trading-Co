Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Ruspert coated screw?

A Ruspert coated screw is a self drilling screw finished with Ruspert, a high-performance coating made from ceramic materials that gives exceptional corrosion resistance. It is used where ordinary zinc plated screws would rust quickly, such as outdoor and coastal installations.

Where are Ruspert coated self drilling screws used?

They are used in outdoor construction, solar panel mounting, HVAC installations and the automotive industry, where screws must withstand harsh environmental conditions without losing their integrity.

Do Ruspert screws need pre-drilling?

No. They have a self drilling tip that drills its own hole and forms the thread in one operation, so there is no need to pre-drill. This makes installation quick and easy.
