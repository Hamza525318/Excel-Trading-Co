Nord-Lock Wedge-Locking Washers

Original Nord-Lock  ·  NL & NLsp  ·  Steel & Stainless Steel 316  ·  M3 to M130

Nord-Lock washers secure bolted joints with tension instead of friction. Each pair has cams on the faces that meet each other and radial serrations on the outer faces that grip the bolt head and the mating surface. Because the cam angle is greater than the thread pitch, any loosening movement has to ride up the cams, which increases bolt tension instead of releasing it. The joint stays tight under severe vibration and dynamic loads. NL vs NLsp — Which One Do You Need?

NL and NLsp are the same wedge-locking system. They differ only in outer diameter (OD), which decides where each is the better fit.

Feature NL (standard) NLsp (enlarged OD)
Outer diameter Standard — sized for counterbore holes per DIN 974 Enlarged — wider contact area under the head or nut
Best for Regular joints, counterbored holes, space-limited designs Large or slotted holes; soft underlying surfaces
Pairs best with Standard hex bolts and nuts Flanged bolts and flanged nuts
Steel size range M3 – M130 M3.5 – M36
Stainless 316 size range M3 – M80 (NLss) M3.5 – M30 (NLspss)
Designation example NL10 fits an M10 bolt NL10sp fits an M10 bolt

Installation Notes

  • ▸ Fit the pre-assembled washer pair cam face to cam face, with the serrated sides against the bolt head and the mating surface. Lubricate the thread and the area under the head or nut before tightening, and use a calibrated torque wrench.
  • ▸ In a through-hole joint use two pairs, one under the head and one under the nut. Turn both fasteners to close the cams before the final tightening, and hold one part still while you tighten the other.
  • ▸ The washer hardness must be higher than the mating surfaces for the serrations to bite. Nord-Lock washers are not recommended on surfaces harder than the washer, on very soft materials such as wood or plastic, on joints that are not locked in place, or where settlement is extreme.
  • ▸ Use NLsp washers with a flanged bolt or nut for large or slotted holes and soft underlying surfaces. Left-hand threads need customised washers.
  • ▸ When loosening, check that sliding occurs between the cam faces. After disassembly, impression marks should be visible on both the fastener and the contact surface.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Nord-Lock NL and NLsp washers?

Both are the same wedge-locking washer pair, made from the same material with the same locking function. The only difference is the outer diameter. NL has the standard outer diameter, designed to fit counterbore holes per DIN 974. NLsp ("sp") has an enlarged outer diameter, intended for large or slotted holes and for soft underlying surfaces, normally used together with a flanged bolt or nut so the clamp load is spread over a wider area. The bolt size equals the washer designation, so NL10 and NL10sp both fit an M10 bolt.

How is a Nord-Lock washer different from a spring washer?

A spring washer relies on spring tension and friction, which vibration or settlement can overcome. A Nord-Lock washer pair locks by tension instead of friction. The cam faces meet each other and the outer serrations grip the bolt head and the mating surface, so any attempt to rotate loose has to ride up the cams, which increases bolt tension instead of releasing it. The locking function is also not affected by lubrication.

Which Nord-Lock washer size do I need for my bolt?

Match the designation to the bolt: NL6 for an M6 bolt, NL10 for M10, NL16 for M16 and so on. Use the size tables on this page for the inner diameter, outer diameter and thickness of each size. Nord-Lock washers are supplied as pre-assembled pairs, so you order pairs, not single washers. If you are not sure whether you need NL or NLsp, send us the joint details and we will advise.

Should I choose steel or stainless steel 316 Nord-Lock washers?

Steel Nord-Lock washers (NL, NLsp) are through-hardened with a Delta Protekt zinc flake coating, are suitable with bolts up to grade 12.9 and give at least 1,000 hours salt spray resistance to ISO 9227. Stainless steel washers (NLss, NLspss) are made from EN 1.4404 (AISI 316L), suit bolts up to A4-80, and cover a wider temperature range, but are not recommended where chlorides or acids are present. Choose stainless where the joint will be exposed to moisture or corrosion, and steel for general high-strength joints. We supply both.

Can Nord-Lock washers be reused?

Nord-Lock washers can normally be reused. As with all fasteners, inspect them for wear before reassembly, make sure they are refitted cam face to cam face, and lubricate the fasteners before reuse to keep friction conditions consistent. After disassembly you should see impression marks on both the fastener and the contact surface, which shows the serrations were gripping.

Do I need one pair or two pairs of washers on a bolted joint?

In a tapped (threaded) hole you use one pair under the bolt head. In a through-hole joint with a nut you need two pairs, one under the bolt head and one under the nut. Always close the cams on both pairs before final tightening, and keep the bolt or nut secured while you tighten the other end.
