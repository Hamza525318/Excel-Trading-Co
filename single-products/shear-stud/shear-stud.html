Frequently Asked Questions

What is a shear stud?

A shear stud, or shear connector, is a headed steel stud welded to a steel beam or deck so that it is cast into the concrete slab above. It transfers shear forces between the steel and the concrete, making them act together as one composite structure, which allows lighter beams and longer spans.

Which sizes of shear stud are available?

Shear studs are available in mild steel in 13, 16, 19, 22 and 25 mm diameters, made to ISO 13918. The quantity per pack varies by size.

Which standard do the shear studs follow?

They are made to ISO 13918, the international standard for studs and ceramic ferrules for arc stud welding, and are made in India.
