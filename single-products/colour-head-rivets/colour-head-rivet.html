Frequently Asked Questions

What is a colour head rivet?

A colour head rivet is a blind rivet with a painted head that matches the colour of the panel it fixes, so the fixing points blend into the facade. Our facade fixing rivets have an aluminium alloy AlMg3.5 rivet body and a stainless steel 1.4301 nail (mandrel), and they are used to fix high pressure laminate (HPL) facade panels to their sub-frame.

Which colours are available?

Colour head rivets can be supplied in all kinds of RAL colours on special request, giving a neat and uniform finish that matches the cladding panels. Share the RAL code of your panels when you enquire.

Why are facade rivets made of aluminium with a stainless nail?

The AlMg3.5 aluminium alloy body is light, corrosion resistant and compatible with aluminium sub-frames, while the stainless steel 1.4301 nail does not rust or stain the facade. Together they give a durable fixing that keeps its appearance outdoors.

What are colour head rivets used for?

They are mainly used to fix high pressure laminate (HPL) facade panels and other cladding panels to aluminium sub-structures on building facades, where the fixing is visible and must match the panel colour.
