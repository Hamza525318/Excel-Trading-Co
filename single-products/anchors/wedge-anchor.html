Frequently Asked Questions

What is a wedge anchor and how does it work?

A wedge anchor, also called a through bolt anchor, is a threaded steel bolt with an expansion clip around its tapered end. It is pushed into a drilled hole in concrete, and when the hexagonal nut is tightened, the tapered end is pulled up into the clip. The clip expands against the hole wall and locks the anchor in place, and it cannot fall off or twist in the hole.

What are wedge anchors used for?

Wedge anchors are used to fix steel constructions, railings, gates, window elements and wooden constructions to concrete. They are a common choice where a strong, permanent fixing is needed and the fixture can be drilled through, because the anchor is installed straight through the item being fixed.

Can a wedge anchor be installed through the fixture?

Yes. Wedge anchors are usually installed through the fixture: the hole is drilled through the item being fixed into the concrete, the anchor is tapped in, and the nut is tightened. This keeps the fixture and the hole perfectly aligned and makes installation quick.

How do I install a wedge anchor correctly?

Drill a hole of the specified diameter and depth, remove the dust from the hole, insert the anchor with the washer and nut fitted, and tighten the nut to the specified torque. Tightening is what expands the clip, so an anchor that is not tightened will not hold. Always follow the anchor manufacturer's data for hole size, depth and torque.
