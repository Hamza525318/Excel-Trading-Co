Frequently Asked Questions

What is a drop-in anchor?

A drop-in anchor is an internally threaded steel anchor that is set into a drilled hole in concrete or natural stone. An internal expansion cone is driven down with a setting tool, which expands the anchor body against the hole wall. The anchor sits flush with the surface and accepts a bolt or threaded rod, so the fixture can be removed and refitted later while the anchor stays in place.

What can drop-in anchors be used to fix?

Drop-in anchors are widely used for overhead and services installations: pipes, ventilation ducts, sprinkler systems, consoles, steel constructions, gratings, cable trays and gates. Because the anchor sits flush and takes a threaded rod, it is especially convenient for hanging services from concrete ceilings.

Which base materials are drop-in anchors suitable for?

Drop-in anchors are designed for solid base materials such as concrete and natural stone, where the expanded body can grip the hole wall firmly. They are not suitable for hollow bricks, blocks or soft masonry; for those, a plug or a chemical anchor is the better choice.

Do I need a special tool to install a drop-in anchor?

Yes. A drop-in anchor is set with a matching setting tool (setting punch), which drives the internal cone down until the anchor is fully expanded. Using the correct tool for the anchor size ensures full expansion; an anchor that is not fully set will not reach its holding capacity.
