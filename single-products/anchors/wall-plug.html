Frequently Asked Questions

What is a nylon wall plug?

A nylon wall plug is a plastic anchor made from polyamide that is pushed into a drilled hole so a wood screw or chipboard screw can be fixed securely into concrete, brick or building panels. As the screw is driven in, the plug expands and presses against the hole wall. Wings on the plug stop it from turning or sliding in the hole while the screw is tightened.

Which materials can wall plugs be used in?

Wall plugs are suitable for concrete, masonry building materials, perforated bricks and light building panels. They are a general-purpose choice for fixing anything that can be fastened with wood screws or chipboard screws, such as shelves, cabinets, fixtures, frames and fittings.

Why are wall plugs made of nylon?

High quality polyamide (nylon) resists weather, ageing, rust and rotting, and it is largely resistant to chemicals. It also has good electrical insulation properties and high tensile and compressive strength, which is why nylon plugs last longer and hold better than cheap plastic plugs.

Which screw should I use with a wall plug?

Use a wood screw or chipboard screw whose diameter matches the plug size, and choose a length equal to the plug length plus the thickness of the item being fixed. A screw that is too thin will not expand the plug fully, and one that is too short will not reach the end of the plug.
