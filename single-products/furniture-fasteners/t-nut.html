Frequently Asked Questions

What is a four prong T-nut?

A four prong T-nut, or tee nut, is a threaded steel insert with a flat flange and four sharp prongs. It is pressed or hammered into a hole drilled in wood, plywood or particle board, and the prongs bite into the material so the nut does not spin. It gives the wood a strong metal thread for a bolt or machine screw.

Which sizes of T-nut are available?

Furniture T-nuts are available from M4 to M10 in steel. Choose the thread size to match the bolt, and drill the hole to suit the T-nut barrel.

Where are T-nuts used?

T-nuts are used in furniture, cabinets, seating, speaker boxes, climbing holds and any wooden assembly where a bolt must be fixed into wood and removed or refitted later. Fitted from the back of the panel, they leave a clean face on the visible side.

How do I install a T-nut?

Drill a hole for the T-nut barrel from the back face of the panel, place the T-nut in the hole and press or tap it in until the flange is flush and the prongs have fully entered the wood. The bolt is then fitted from the other side.
