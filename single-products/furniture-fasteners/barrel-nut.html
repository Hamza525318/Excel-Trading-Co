Frequently Asked Questions

What is a barrel nut?

A barrel nut, also called a cross dowel nut, is a cylindrical steel nut with a threaded hole across its body. It is fitted into a hole drilled in the face of a panel, and a bolt passes through the adjoining panel into it, pulling the two parts tightly together. Barrel nuts are a standard knock-down fitting for furniture that is assembled and taken apart.

Which sizes of barrel nut are available?

Barrel nuts are available in M6 and M8 thread sizes, in steel. Choose the size to match the connecting bolt used in your furniture design.

Where are barrel nuts used?

Barrel nuts are used in flat-pack and knock-down furniture such as beds, cots, wardrobes, tables and shelving, and in other hardware fittings where two panels or rails must be joined strongly at right angles and still be easy to dismantle.

How is a barrel nut installed?

Drill a hole across the panel for the barrel nut and a hole through the joining part for the bolt. Insert the barrel nut, turn it with a screwdriver in its slot until the threaded hole lines up with the bolt hole, then insert and tighten the bolt.
